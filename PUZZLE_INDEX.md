## Puzzle Descriptions

### Puzzle 1: Wells in Africa (Word Grid)
Grid of letters with water droplet symbols hiding certain letters. Clues provided at the bottom:
- Left column: "A cold noise?", "A natural disaster", "MrBeast went to one in Greenville, NC", "Deceive", "Rats! (2 wds.)", "A certain Midwesterner", "A child's toy (2 wds., Hyph.)"
- Right column: "Upset", "An item you can buy in MrBeast's store", "A US landmark (2 wds.)", "A type of boat", "A literary outlaw (2 wds.)", "What you might say when you finish this!"
- Solve: Fill in the hidden letters (behind water droplets) using crossword-style clues

### Puzzle 2: 600 Strangers (LIFECHANG Sudoku)
Standard 9x9 Sudoku variant using letters L, I, F, E, C, H, A, N, G instead of 1-9.
- Letters spell "LIFE" vertically on right side + "CHANGE" horizontally at bottom
- Some cells pre-filled with letters
- Solve like normal Sudoku but with letters

### Puzzle 4: $1-$500K Experiences (Anagram Places)
"I went all over the world for these experiences. Where did I go?"
- Colored letter strips with scrambled place names
- Each strip has letters in different colors (significant?)
- Names appear to be anagrams of real places/streets

### Puzzle 5: Pokemon Go (Cage Bars)
"The Guy Who Will Do Anything has been caged by these Pokémon. He cannot get out."
- Large grid with a running figure icon in the center
- "Cage Bars" with numbers: 142, 200, 261, 343, 439, 696, 803, 947, 961, 1006, 138, 148, 175, 266, 276, 441, 791, 852
- Each bar has a specific shape that fits into the grid
- Numbers likely correspond to Pokémon Pokédex numbers

### Puzzle 6: Wilderness (Tent Placement)
16x16 grid with trees (represented by circle-on-stick icons) and numbers.
Rules:
- Place exactly one tent horizontally or vertically adjacent to each tree
- No tent can touch any other tent, even diagonally
- Numbers outside the grid show how many tents are in that row/column
- Numbers inside the grid are standard (1-9) and need to be part of the solve

### Puzzle 7: Adopted Dogs (Venn Diagram)
100 cartoon dogs, each with varying features. A 5-circle Venn diagram shows the categories:
- Whose ears are up?
- Who's got a toy bone?
- Who's got spots?
- Who's wearing a collar?
- Who's wiggling their tail?
- Count dogs in each Venn region to extract numbers/letters

### Puzzle 8: Pyramids (Pyramid Crossword)
Pyramid-shaped grid with crossword-style clues on both sides.
- Left side clues read down-left, right side clues read down-right
- Clues include: "Vowel that's not U", "Bedeck", "Convertible such as a drophead coupé", etc.
- Notable clue references: "Ben 10 series", "Arina Tanemura manga", "NASCAR event"

### Puzzle 9: Circle (Geometric)
Two geometric circles (tessellated/triangulated like geodesic domes):
- Top circle has red-highlighted triangular section
- Bottom circle has blue-highlighted triangular section
- Three dots in the middle (ellipsis = more circles?)
- Bottom text: "ZXPCHAIRSQUORUMSFLAMINGOPUSHCONVEXOFWEHIRPLED"
- This string contains hidden words: CHAIRS, QUORUMS, FLAMINGO, PUSH, CONVEX, OF, WE, HIRPLED
- The colored triangle sections on the circles likely map to letters in those words
