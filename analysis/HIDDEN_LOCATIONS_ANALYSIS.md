## 1. THE MECHANISM: What "Hidden Words" Means

In crossword puzzle design, "hidden words" (also called "embedded words") means that a word from a specific category is spelled out by **consecutive letters** within a longer entry answer. The hidden word is a contiguous substring of the theme entry.

**Example:** The word OSLO hidden in "COLOSSAL" would NOT work (O-S-L-O is not consecutive in C-O-L-O-S-S-A-L). But PERU hidden in "SUPERUSER" DOES work: S-U-**P-E-R-U**-S-E-R.

**Key rules for this puzzle:**
- Each hidden location name appears as consecutive letters within ONE theme entry answer
- The hidden word must span at least part of the answer (it's a substring)
- 11 location names are hidden across all theme entries collectively
- The locations are real places "somewhere around the world" (per the 9-word sentence)
- The 11 words go into the staircase grid (bottom-right of crossword PDF)

**Important distinction:** The P4 cities (TOLEDO, DENVER, etc.) are DIRECT crossword entries placed in the grid. The 11 hidden locations are DIFFERENT - they are embedded within the letter sequences of the longest theme entries.


**NOTE:** These are APPROXIMATE. Precise digitization from a high-resolution image is needed.

### What This Tells Us About Location Lengths
- Most hidden locations are 4-7 letters long
- No very long locations (8+ letters) appear to be needed
- This is consistent with the Tier 1 "easily hideable" locations (IRAN=4, ROME=4, OMAN=4, PERU=4, CUBA=4, LIMA=4, BALI=4, MALI=4, DOVER=5, etc.)

### Interlocking Constraints
Where rows overlap vertically:
- The shared letter must match between the two location names
- Example: If Row 3 = LIMA and Row 4 = OMAN, and they share a column at position where LIMA's last letter (A) meets OMAN's first letter (O), they DON'T interlock (A ≠ O). But if the overlap is at LIMA[2]=M and OMAN[0]=O, those also don't match.
- The interlocking pattern strongly constrains which locations can appear together.
