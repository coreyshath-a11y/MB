### How It Connects to the 9 Variety Puzzles

The 9 variety puzzles produce 9 words that form a sentence:
```
EVERY CHALLENGE LEADS [?] LOCATION NAME ONE AROUND [?]
```

**Theory 1:** The sentence IS the instruction for solving the crossword
- "Every challenge leads to [a] location. Name one around the [world/crossword]"
- Maybe each puzzle's answer is actually a location, and you find those locations in the crossword

**Theory 2:** The 9 words are clues for 9 crossword entries
- But the words don't match crossword entry lengths (jamesrocks4512 noted EVERY doesn't fit line 1's 4-letter slot)

**Theory 3:** The sentence is a riddle whose answer helps solve the crossword
- "The word RIDDLE keeps popping up everywhere"
- The answer to the riddle might be a key word

**Theory 4:** The crossword is pre-filled or fill-able from the title/theme
- "I WROTE A PUZZLE FOR JIMMY" might be the theme
- Each theme entry contains the word in a different form
- 11 hidden words within theme entries spell the final code

### Circled Cells
The crossword has circles in certain cells. In meta-puzzles, circled letters typically:
1. Spell out a message when read in order
2. Mark where hidden words begin or end
3. Indicate letters to extract from theme entries

The answer to 167 Across would be read from the circled cells after filling in the grid.
